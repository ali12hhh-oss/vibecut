# Third-party notices

VibeCut Pro must only ship free and legally permitted dependencies and assets.

## Current policy

- Flutter/Dart packages must be checked before release for license compatibility.
- Fonts should preferably use the SIL Open Font License or another permissive license.
- Stickers, LUTs, overlays, and transitions must not be copied from commercial packs unless redistribution is explicitly allowed.
- User-downloaded assets remain the user's responsibility unless they are distributed by the app.

## Before publishing

Create a release audit table for every bundled asset:

| Asset path | Source | Author | License | Commercial use | Redistribution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

If the license cannot be proven, remove the asset from the release build.
